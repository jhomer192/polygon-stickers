✂︎ Stickers
Install

Pick any image — photo, screenshot, meme. Then tap the background to erase it.

Higher = grabs more similar pixels per tap. Flat backgrounds: low. Photos: high.

Auto-remove downloads a ~30 MB on-device model the first time you tap it. Runs entirely on your phone after that.

Make photo stickers in your browser

Choose a photo, tap the background with the wand to erase it, or use automatic background removal. Refine the cutout, crop to the subject, then copy or save your sticker.

Image editing happens on your device. Automatic removal downloads a model; copy and sharing options depend on your browser.
